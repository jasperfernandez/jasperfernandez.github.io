About

A bit of context.

I'm a full-stack developer who focuses on building things that actually work well. Not the flashiest or most trendy approach — just clean architecture, clear code, and systems that hold up under real usage.

Most of my work involves designing and building web applications end-to-end: data modeling, API design, server-side logic, and the interfaces people use every day. I care about maintainability because I've inherited enough codebases to know what happens when nobody does.

Outside of my core work, I'm spending time learning about AI engineering — understanding how to integrate machine learning into practical applications — and cybersecurity, because building reliable systems means understanding how they break. Neither is my specialty yet, but both are shaping how I think about software.
